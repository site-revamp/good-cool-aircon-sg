Helpful information

Questions before you arrange a service?

Use these starting points to understand the different types of aircon care.

What is the difference between general servicing and chemical wash?

General servicing is routine maintenance. A chemical wash is a deeper cleaning option. The appropriate treatment depends on the unit's condition and should be assessed before work is agreed.

When might a chemical overhaul be needed?

A chemical overhaul is a more comprehensive cleaning procedure. It is not automatically required for every unit. Ask the technician to explain why it is recommended and what the work includes.

Should I request a gas top-up if the aircon is not cold?

Not necessarily. Poor cooling can have several causes. Request troubleshooting first so the cause can be identified before deciding whether refrigerant work is appropriate.

What should I confirm before booking?

Confirm the service scope, number and type of units, any inspection or call-out fee, the total price, availability and whether additional work requires your approval.
