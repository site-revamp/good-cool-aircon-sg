Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you arrange a service.

How often should I service my home aircon?

GoodCool's website recommends servicing residential units approximately every three to four months. Actual frequency depends on usage, environment and the condition of the unit.

What is the difference between general servicing and chemical wash?

General servicing covers routine cleaning and checks. Chemical wash is a deeper cleaning process intended for more substantial dirt and build-up. Ask whether it is appropriate for your unit.

When should I consider a chemical overhaul?

A chemical overhaul is a more comprehensive process involving dismantling and cleaning. It should be recommended based on the condition of the system rather than treated as a routine requirement.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. GoodCool provides an online appointment request page. The business says it will confirm the selected slot or contact customers to arrange a nearby available time.

Does GoodCool serve locations across Singapore?

GoodCool states that it provides aircon servicing island-wide in Singapore. Confirm appointment availability for your location when booking.
